How it plays

Shop

Every round you get a private board of towers and units. Buy what fits your plan, reroll what doesn't.

Merge

Three copies of the same tower or unit fuse into a stronger one. Collect, combine, climb.

Maze

Towers block the path. Wall your deck into a maze so enemy armies walk the long way past your guns.

Attack

Your army marches through every other player's maze. Whatever survives hits their core.

Install

Windows

  1. Download the zip.
  2. Right-click it → Extract All.
  3. Run KillingTheEnemiesTD.exe.
  4. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears: More info → Run anyway.

Found a bug? Run KillingTheEnemiesTD.console.exe instead and screenshot the log window.

Mac

  1. Download the zip and double-click it.
  2. Right-click the app → Open → Open (first time only).
  3. If macOS says the app is “damaged”, open Terminal in that folder and run:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "Killing the enemiesTD.app"

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Play with friends

Everyone opens Steam and logs in. You don't need to own anything – the game just uses Steam to connect you.

One player clicks Play online → Host (Steam).

Everyone else clicks Play online and joins the game from the list. Windows and Mac players can play together.

Just want to try it? Pick Practice vs bots from the main menu.

Playtest build: a player who disconnects is out of the match, and if the host quits the match ends for everyone.
